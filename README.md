## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **16 Note Polyphony** — Create complex soundscapes with 128 ready-to-use presets.
- **2-Oscillator Instrument** — Enjoy rich sounds with ring modulation and FM modulation controls.
- **Four Types of Filters** — Shape your sound with a variety of filtering options.
- **Two LFOs** — Synchronize with your sequencer for dynamic sound modulation.
- **Automation Capabilities** — Fine-tune your parameters for precise control over your sound.

### Advanced Features:
- **Arupeije Patterns** — Generate automatic patterns easily.
- **Multiple Effects** — Enhance your sound with delay, chorus, flanger, and more.
- **Legato and Portamento Modes** — Create fluid transitions between notes.
